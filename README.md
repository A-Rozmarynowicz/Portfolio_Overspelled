## About the project
"Overspelled" is a large-scale, interdisciplinary, hobby project. Though still under development, this commercial video game is a result of currently two years, or over 1 400 hours of programming, drawing, researching, testing, brainstorming, failing and starting over, to name a few.

The game has it's webpage on the largest video game platform in the world, Steam, and it can be viewed here:
[https://store.steampowered.com/app/3425570/Overspelled/](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3425570/Overspelled/)

"Overspelled" demo is currently available for free. To access it, please download the Steam desktop app and navigate to my game's webpage. I encourage you to give it a try!

## Relevance to []'s studentship
I believe that the journey of progressing a project from the point where it was just a vague idea, to the point where it is accessible to people around the world has enabled me to develop skills and traits that are of great value in CERN's laboratories:

- **Teamwork**: throughout the journey I have collaborated with game testers, artists, musicians and a lawyer. At every point I needed to listen, explain, ask questions and brainstorm with talented people in a supportive setting. Being a good teamplayer will play a crucial role when working with professionals from all around the world.

 - **Perserveance**: staying focused on a single task for two years, when commercial success is rather unlikely and the only reward is my own satisfaction have strengthened my determination to a great extend. I am now ready to be part of a project that requires a non-stop focus and full commitment all throughout the process.

 - **Creativity**: making a video game means constantly coming up with new ideas, not only on what to create but also how to implement it. Only a very small part of these concepts ever make it to the final product, hence the creative skills need to be trained thouroughly. I believe that this trait will be usefull when solving problems at the forefront of scientific discovery.

 - **Patience**: building complex systems piece by piece, fixing bugs that I made a year earlier, or reworking large parts of the project have definitely put my patience to a test. I think this trait is necessary when working on a very demanding engineering project in Geneva.

- **Project Management**: developing, managing and maintaining a large programming project grants experience when it comes to careful design decisions and structuring a system. I learned that all minor faults and negligence in a project structure will cause trobles in the future, and I will pay great attention to details when working on a high-stakes project.
